# T04-007 · Add a QR code dependency for asset labels and invite links

Status: OPEN
Requested by: T04 · Wave: W2
Target: `apps/web/package.json` (owner: T01)
Kind: dependency

## Problem
Two features need QR images, and no QR library is installed:
- The asset QR label and sheet: `GET /api/v1/assets/:id/qr.png` and `(admin)/assets/qr-sheet`.
- The invite and reset share panel.

## Proposed change
Add `qrcode` and `@types/qrcode`. Render a PNG in `assets/[id]/qr.png/route.ts` with `QRCode.toBuffer(url)`, and render an SVG in `SharePanel` and on the QR sheet.

## Workaround in place
- The QR route returns an explicit placeholder.
- The share panel shows the link and its short code in large type, together with copy and WhatsApp actions.
- The QR sheet page is a placeholder that is listed under Gaps.

## Impact if rejected
Field users cannot scan printed labels from the web app.

# T14-003 · Register the maintenance and low-stock jobs in the worker

Status: OPEN
Requested by: T14 · Wave: W6
Target: `apps/worker/src/jobs/index.ts` (owner: T05)
Kind: other

## Problem

T14 owns `apps/worker/src/jobs/maintenance.dueScan.ts` and `apps/worker/src/jobs/stock.lowScan.ts`. Both export `handler(ctx, job)` and have their own tests. The worker loads peer job modules through `PEER_JOB_MODULES` in `apps/worker/src/jobs/index.ts:205`. That map does not list these two jobs, so `loadJobHandlers` gives them the explicit no-op handler. Preventive work orders and low-stock notifications are therefore never produced at runtime.

## Proposed change

Add two entries to `PEER_JOB_MODULES` in `apps/worker/src/jobs/index.ts`:

```ts
  "maintenance.dueScan": () => import("./maintenance.dueScan.js"),
  "stock.lowScan": () => import("./stock.lowScan.js"),
```

## Workaround in place

None at runtime. The handlers are tested directly in `apps/worker/src/jobs/{maintenance.dueScan,stock.lowScan}.test.ts`, and the core functions they call (`maintenance.runDueScan`, `parts.runLowScan`) are tested in core.

## Impact if rejected

ERP-05 and ERP-06 automation does not run: no automatic preventive work orders and no low-stock alerts. The office can still see due plans and low levels on the web pages.

## Assumptions
- KPI "in progress" means work orders in `EN_ROUTE`, `ON_SITE`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `ON_HOLD`. "Overdue rental" means past `plannedEnd` plus the organisation grace. A critical unassigned report is a `CRITICAL` report with no assigned, non-terminal work order.
- "Stale device" means pending mutations or uploads older than `staleSyncAlertHours`. Media count as pending after 24 h. Diagnostics are listed for 30 days.
- Attention resolutions are stored as `ActivityEvent` rows with the deterministic id `attention:<kind>:<id>`, because the db schemas have no resolution fields (T09-001). A stale device reappears only if it goes stale again after the resolution.
- Rentals are counted in the cost period by `closedAt ?? actualEnd`. Rent-in rentals add cost, and rent-out rentals add revenue.
- The ops and costs DTOs live in core as TypeScript interfaces. The web imports them with `import type` only (T09-001).
- The Ops Center opens its own socket and renders react-map-gl directly, because the T04 `useRealtime` and `MapView` seams expose no events, ref or error hook (T09-002).
- The KPI-to-alert mapping is: open reports → critical unassigned; unassigned or SLA at risk → SLA alerts; rentals → overdue rental; on duty → stale device; done today → all alerts.
- `GettingStartedCard` reads `settings.onboarding.{dismissedAt,steps}` leniently, because T20 owns that shape. The step labels live in `ops.json`.
- Clicking an entity on the map navigates with `window.location.assign`, because detail routes belong to other tasks.
- The operator trail covers the current day, which keeps it inside the 2 h trail requirement. Gaps of more than 10 minutes (location-buffer trimming, phone off) break the line and show a note.

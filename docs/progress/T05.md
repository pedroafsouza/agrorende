### Cleanup behaviour
- **Media.**
  - PENDING media older than 24 h is reported per organisation as `attention.media_pending`.
  - PENDING media older than 30 days becomes FAILED with a new `syncVersion`.
  - Media soft-deleted more than 7 days ago has its stored objects (`storageKey`, `thumbnailKey`, variants) deleted, then its row.
  - Under `MEDIA_ROOT`: upload sessions in `.uploads/<id>` older than 24 h are removed, as are `.tmp/` entries and stray `*.tmp` files older than 1 h.
- **Location pings.** The TTL index removes them after 30 days. `locations.prune` deletes any rows left over, in chunks of 1 000.
- **Processed mutations.** APPLIED rows are kept 30 days and REJECTED rows 90 days.
- **Diagnostics.** Rows are kept 30 days, and payloads above 256 KB are trimmed.
- **Tombstones.**
  - Applies to synced collections, meaning those whose schema has both `syncVersion` and `deletedAt`. `users` and `media` are excluded.
  - Rows with `deletedAt` older than 90 days are deleted.
  - Before deleting, the job raises `sync_counters.horizonVersion` (using `$max`) to the highest `syncVersion` it deletes.
- **Exports.** Downloads expire after 7 days. `exports.build` expires them on every run and deletes the stored file.

## Assumptions
- **Placement of the BullMQ runtime.** It lives in `apps/worker/src/jobs/index.ts` (`createJobRuntime`). The T01 files `jobRegistry.ts` and `processors.ts` stay as they are and remain the registry. `queues.ts` is no longer imported by `index.ts` and can be removed by T01.
- **Peer job files.** Optional per-file modules, loaded by name, give each peer owner a registration point without editing T05 files.
- **Apple configuration.** The worker treats `APPLE_TEAM_ID`, `APPLE_KEY_ID` and `APPLE_PRIVATE_KEY` as the switch. If none is set, it logs a warning. If only some are set, or `APPLE_CLIENT_ID` is missing while the secrets are set, it fails at startup. `APPLE_CLIENT_ID` has a non-secret default in `.env.example`.
- **Push job input.** `notifications.push` accepts `notificationIds` or `pushNotificationIds`, because the payload name T02's dispatcher uses was not fixed. Without ids, it scans unpushed notifications from the last 24 h.
- **Export locale.** The CSV locale comes from `?locale=` or `Accept-Language`.
- **Duplicate export requests.** A repeated request reuses the QUEUED or RUNNING job with the same entity and filters.
- **The `[entity].csv` route.** The pathname is also matched inside `exports/[id]`, so `/exports/assets.csv` works whichever segment Next.js routes it to.
- **API authentication.** The routes use T04's `authenticate` and `route` from `apps/web/src/lib/core.server.ts`, so there is one JWT check for every route.
- **Malformed `auth.appleRevoke` payloads** are dropped and logged, not retried.

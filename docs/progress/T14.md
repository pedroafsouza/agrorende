## Assumptions

- The office may drive a level below zero with an `OUT` or `ADJUST` movement. Real counts sometimes lag the system, and blocking the office would hide the discrepancy. Only the technician's job lines are blocked by `WO_PART_NOT_IN_VAN_STOCK`.
- A FIELD user sees only their own van and the warehouses. They do not see colleagues' vans.
- The strict external-cost rule is enforced in the route through `setExternalCostFromCompleted`. The older `setExternalCost` stays permissive so that the frozen T07 test keeps passing (T14-002).
- The `lastDoneAt` day of a maintenance plan is read in the organisation time zone (`-03:00` for the seed), so a plan done "today" is not due again today.
- On the device, a catalog line for more than the van holds is blocked before queueing, so the server rejection only happens when the mirror was stale.
- "Hold for waiting parts" is offered only to the assignee of an `IN_PROGRESS` job, because that is the only state from which a technician may put a job on hold.
- The web panels use a nested `PartsMessages` provider until `WorkOrdersIntl` loads the `parts` namespace (T14-004).

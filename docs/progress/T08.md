### Core (`packages/core/src/modules/rentals/`)

  - the signer rules: check-out must be SIGNED; check-in may be ABSENT, or REFUSED with a reason, and either one opens a 48 h dispute window with an attention activity;

  - meter readings: `METER_READING_LOWER`, `METER_READING_JUMP`, and a meter-replaced bypass that needs a photo;
  - accessory deltas;

  - `assets.setCustody`, `assets.applyReadings` and `touchDependents`;

## Assumptions

- A damage input with `matchStatus: MATCHED` and a `matchedDamageId` confirms an open damage. It is not stored as a new damage.
- Damages recorded at check-out are pre-existing and are never charged.
- `meterReplaced: true` with a meter photo bypasses the lower-reading and jump guards. There is no other way to confirm a jump (see change request T08-001).
- The check-in jump limit is the elapsed hours since check-out plus one hour.
- `inspection.submit` requires a `rentalId`.
- A check-in by a different agent writes an attention activity that records both agents. The signer identities are stored on each inspection.
- An ABSENT or REFUSED signature together with new damages writes `rental.damagesNotAcknowledged`, with a 48 h dispute window.
- Every manual charge edit and every recompute bumps `chargesVersion`. The automatic charges written at check-in do not.
- Close is blocked with `VALIDATION_FAILED` and details `{reason: "damageHeld", damageIds}` while any damage of the rental is DISPUTED or UNCLEAR.
- `checkEligibility` always evaluates the OUTBOUND direction.
- PDFs are rendered inline on request and are not cached as media.
- An overdue escalation also stamps `dueSoonNotifiedAt`, so a due-soon notification is never sent after an overdue one.
- The organisation grace period defaults to 2 h and the due-soon window to 24 h when the settings document has no values.
- Tests mock `seams.js`, using `importOriginal` for `createFieldReportFromDamage`, rather than `fieldReports/service.ts`, because the rentals code calls through the seam (R12 deviation).
- Web: office roles see the full CPF/CNPJ, formatted. Damage actions and charge editing are enabled only when the rental is RETURNED. DISCOUNT lines are entered as positive amounts and stored negative.
- Mobile:
  - Check-in reuses the check-out template snapshot.
  - Fuel is entered in eighths.
  - Asset identity can be confirmed by eye as well as by QR.
  - New rentals are OUTBOUND, priced per DAY.

# AgroRende mark and logo

This document records why the AgroRende mark looks the way it does, how it is built and
how to use it. The palette and the outdoor rules of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.7 "Campo
claro" apply throughout; where this document and §3.7 disagree, §3.7 wins.

All artwork is generated by `packages/design-tokens/brand/generate.py` (pure geometry, no
font needed at render time, byte-identical output on every run) and rasterised by
`pnpm --filter landing brand`. Never edit the SVGs, the PNGs or `BrandLogo.tsx` by hand.

## 1. The brief

AgroRende is field service for farm-machinery dealers and rental companies in Brazil. A
technician reports a breakdown from the field, offline if need be; the office sees it
live; the business learns what each machine really costs and earns. "Rende" means *it
yields*: the product turns field work into returns.

The mark has to carry three ideas — **the field**, **service on the machine** and **a
live report that yields something** — and it has to survive the places it actually
lives: a 16 px browser tab, a 36 px collapsed admin sidebar, a phone home screen cropped
into a circle or a squircle by Android, a dusty screen in full sun, and a single-colour
stamp on a printed service order.

### What was wrong with the previous mark

The previous mark was a white twelve-tooth gear ring with a small orange leaf inside a
green tile.

- **Cliché.** Gear plus leaf is the stock symbol for "agri-tech" and "green industry"; it
  says nothing about service, reports or yield.
- **Busy.** Teeth, a hole, a leaf and a vein are four ideas in 64 units. At 16 px the
  teeth turn into a fuzzy ring and the leaf into a brown smudge.
- **Colour-dependent.** Terra orange (#E8590C) and Mata green (#2B8A3E) have almost the
  same luminance (about 1.2:1), so the leaf vanished in greyscale, for colour-blind users
  and in one-colour print.
- **No letter.** Nothing tied the symbol to the name.

## 2. Directions explored

Each direction was drawn on the 64-unit grid and rendered at 16, 24, 32, 48 and 160 px,
on white and on dark, in colour and in greyscale. Scores run from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong).

| Direction | Meaning | Distinctive | 16 px | 24 px | 48 px | One colour | Adaptive and maskable | Free of cliché | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Cabeceira: furrow A with a report node on the headland row | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **37** |
| B. Sol no sulco: open furrow A, sun at the vanishing point | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 28 |
| C. Garra: staggered tractor-tyre lugs forming a rising chevron | 4 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 29 |
| D. Pin and rows: a map pin holding crop rows | 3 | 1 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 24 |
| E. Signal over rows: sync arcs above furrows | 3 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 20 |
| F. R de rendimento: an R whose leg is a rising yield curve | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 26 |
| Previous gear and leaf, for reference | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 15 |

### A. Cabeceira (chosen)

Two furrows run toward the horizon and meet: they *are* the two legs of a capital A, each
narrowing as it recedes. The crossbar is the **cabeceira**, the headland row a tractor
turns on at the end of a field. On that row sits a **node**: a white ring around a Terra
core. The node is the same circle the product uses for a field report on the map (§3.7:
reports are circles, work orders squares, assets diamonds), so the mark literally shows a
report sitting in a field. Read quickly, it is an A with a live dot: the first letter of
the name, a status light that says "live", and a point of yield on the row.

- The white ring is what makes the Terra core visible: orange sits on white (about
  3.6:1), not on green (about 1.2:1), so the dot survives greyscale and colour blindness.
  In one colour the core becomes a hole and the ring still reads.
- The silhouette is a closed triangle with a solid apex and wide feet, so circle and
  squircle masks never cut into it, and it never reads as a figure.
- One idea, two shapes (the A and the node), one accent. At 16 px it is still an A with a
  dot.

### B. Sol no sulco

The first sketch of the furrow idea: the furrows stop short of their vanishing point and a
Terra sun sits where they would meet. Strong at 16 px, but at 96 px and above the open legs
under a round "head" read as a human pictogram (a restroom sign), and the sun on green
disappears in greyscale. Rejected; its best idea, the converging furrows, survives in A.

### C. Garra

Tractor-tyre lugs, staggered left and right, stacked into a rising chevron: machinery plus
growth. Distinctive at 48 px, but four diagonal bars blur into a zigzag at 16 px, the
shape carries no letter, and stacked chevrons already belong to military ranks and
"upgrade" buttons.

### D. Pin and rows

A map pin with crop rows inside. The meaning is clear, but the pin is the default symbol
of every delivery, logistics and field-service app; it would make AgroRende look like all
of them.

### E. Signal over rows

Wi-Fi-style arcs over furrows for "live". The arcs merge at 16 and 24 px, and the image
promises connectivity, which is exactly what the field often lacks (AgroRende is built to
work offline).

### F. R de rendimento

An R whose leg becomes a rising yield curve. The curve reads as a finance or trading
logo, and an R at 16 px is a blob; A is the more legible letter and it starts the name.

## 3. Construction

All values are in units of the 64 × 64 master grid. The grid has a **4-unit module**, which
is one pixel at 16 px.

```
y  0 ┌──────────────────────────────┐  tile 64 × 64, corner radius 14 (22 %)
y  9 │             ▄▄▄▄             │  flat apex from x 30 to 34
y 16 │            ▟▌  ▐▙            │  counter apex at (32, 16)
     │           ▟▌    ▐▙           │  inner edges (18, 53) → (32, 16)
y 42 │          ▟▬▬ (●) ▬▬▙         │  crossbar y 39.5–44, node at (32, 41.75)
     │         ▟▌          ▐▙       │  outer edges (10, 53) → (30, 9)
y 53 │        ▀▀▀▀        ▀▀▀▀      │  feet x 10–18 and 46–54, baseline y 53
y 64 └──────────────────────────────┘
```

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Tile | 64 × 64, corner radius 14, Mata 600 (#2B8A3E) |
| Furrows (legs of the A) | Outer edge from the foot at x 10 to the flat apex at (30, 9); inner edge from the foot at x 18 to the counter apex at (32, 16). Each leg is 8 units wide at the foot and narrows toward the apex. |
| Apex | Flat, 4 units wide, echoing the flat apex of Geist's A in the wordmark. |
| Crossbar (headland) | y 39.5 to 44 (4.5 units), spanning the counter. |
| Node | Centre (32, 41.75); white ring radius 6; Terra 600 (#E8590C) core radius 3.5. |
| Corners | Every corner of the A is rounded by 1 unit (drawn as a 2-unit round-join stroke). |
| Glyph bounds | x 9 to 55, y 8 to 54; centre y 31. |

### Favicon drawing (16 to 32 px)

Below 32 px the master's details fall between pixels. `agrorende-favicon.svg` redraws the
same mark on a 16-unit grid: apex at y 2, crossbar at y 10 to 11.5, baseline at y 14,
furrows a little heavier (3.25 px at the foot), node ring radius 2.25 and core 1.2, tile
radius 3.5. It is used for `icon.svg` in the admin web app and the site and for the 16 and
32 px entries of `favicon.ico`; the 48 px entry uses the master.

### Wordmark and lockup

- "AgroRende" is set in **Geist Sans SemiBold (600)**, the UI face of the admin web app and
  the site (§3.7), converted to outlines so it renders identically everywhere.
- Kerning comes from the font's own `kern` feature; tracking is −1.2 %.
- The wordmark is one colour. The camel case already separates *Agro* and *Rende*; a second
  colour on "Rende" would compete with the Terra node, which is the only accent.
- Cap height is 28 units (7/16 of the tile), centred on the glyph's centre (baseline y 45).
- The gap between tile and wordmark is 16 units, a quarter of the tile. The lockup is
  287 × 64 units.

## 4. Clear space and minimum sizes

- **Clear space:** keep a quarter of the tile height (16 units at master size) free on every
  side of the mark and of the lockup.
- **Mark:** 16 px minimum on screen. Use the favicon drawing from 16 to 32 px and the master
  above 32 px. In print, 6 mm minimum.
- **Lockup:** 96 px wide (about 21 px tall) minimum on screen, 25 mm wide in print. Below
  that, use the mark alone.

## 5. Colour variants and files

| File in `packages/design-tokens/brand/` | Use |
|---|---|
| `agrorende-mark.svg` | Full colour: Mata 600 tile, white A and ring, Terra 600 core. The default everywhere, on light and dark backgrounds. |
| `agrorende-mark-mono.svg` | Mono dark or mono light: one colour, no tile, `currentColor`; the core is a hole in the node. Graphite 900 on light, white on dark; for stamps, PDFs and one-ink print. |
| `agrorende-mark-reversed.svg` | Reversed: a one-colour tile with the A knocked out, `currentColor`. Use where the tile shape must stay but only one ink is available. |
| `agrorende-favicon.svg` | Full colour, hinted for 16 to 32 px. |
| `agrorende-logo.svg` | Lockup for light backgrounds: wordmark in Graphite 900 (#1C1F23). |
| `agrorende-logo-dark.svg` | Lockup for dark backgrounds: wordmark in white. |
| `app-icon.svg` | iOS app icon: full-bleed Mata square (the system rounds the corners), glyph at 90 % of master size. |
| `adaptive-foreground.svg` | Android adaptive icon foreground: transparent, glyph fitted inside the 66 dp safe circle of the 108 dp canvas with a 6 % margin; the Mata background colour is set in `apps/mobile/app.config.ts`. Works with circle, squircle and rounded-square masks. |
| `splash-icon.svg` | Splash screen: the full-colour tile. |

In the admin web app, `BrandMark` and `BrandLogo` (`apps/web/src/components/brand/`) render
the same geometry inline; the wordmark follows the theme's `--ar-text` colour, so it is
Graphite in the light theme and white in the dark theme. The site's `Logo` component swaps
the light and dark lockup files.

## 6. Do and don't

**Do**

- Use the generated files and components, and regenerate them from `generate.py`.
- Keep the node Terra on white. It is the only orange in the mark.
- Place the full-colour mark on white, on Graphite 50 to 100 surfaces, on dark Graphite
  surfaces, and on calm areas of field photography.
- Use the one-colour mark when only one ink is available.

**Don't**

- Don't recolour the tile: no Terra tile, no gradient, no other green.
- Don't put the Terra core directly on green or remove its white ring.
- Don't add a gear, a leaf, a sun or a pin; the node is the only symbol.
- Don't rotate, skew, outline, shadow or distort the mark.
- Don't set the wordmark in another typeface, give "Rende" a second colour, or rebuild it
  from live text.
- Don't use the master drawing below 32 px; use the favicon drawing.
- Don't crop the glyph out of the tile for app icons; the adaptive foreground already sizes
  it for every mask.
